## English

Chapter 2 of the series **[Discernimiento humano y creatividad soberana mediada por IA](https://github.com/SandraAcevedoA/serie-discernimiento-creatividad)** (*Human discernment and sovereign creativity, mediated by AI*): a three-hour applied workshop taught in Spanish at [The Multiverse School](https://themultiverse.school/classes/290).

**Central question**

> What can I do when the solution designed for me does not exist, is unavailable, is unaffordable, or simply does not fit my reality?

This repository mirrors the final participant-facing material for the workshop already published at The Multiverse School. The series repository explains how this chapter connects with the other two.

`interactive/recorrido-web/index.html` is self-contained. Download it and open it in a modern browser. External reference links and Quartz graphs still require an internet connection.

### Provenance and credits

This chapter has no source course. It was developed for the series as an applied workshop on inventiveness under material and technological constraints. Its conceptual grounding includes functional fixedness, the Generic Parts Technique, goal-derived categorization, chunk decomposition, constraint relaxation, analogical transfer, technological disobedience and clean-room reimplementation. Sources and credits are included in the workshop materials and linked references.

The illustrations used in the artifact are original workshop graphics or reinterpretations created for the workshop. The documentary photograph by Ernesto Oroza is **not included** in this repository; the artifact uses an original illustration of the documented object and links to Oroza's archive as reference.

### Procedencia y créditos

Este capítulo no tiene un curso fuente. Fue desarrollado para la serie como un workshop aplicado de inventiva bajo restricciones materiales y tecnológicas. Su grounding conceptual incluye fijación funcional, Generic Parts Technique, categorización derivada de metas, chunk decomposition, constraint relaxation, transferencia analógica, desobediencia tecnológica y reimplementación clean room. Las fuentes y créditos están incluidos en los materiales y referencias enlazadas.

Las ilustraciones del artefacto son gráficos originales del workshop o reinterpretaciones creadas para él. La fotografía documental de Ernesto Oroza **no está incluida** en este repositorio; el artefacto usa una ilustración original del objeto documentado y enlaza al archivo de Oroza como referencia.
